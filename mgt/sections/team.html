Organizational Structure

Workplace Culture

SoHara intends to be a quick moving, flat hierarchy organization. In order to fully leverage the small team size, the team must be dedicated to quickly completing tasks and identifying problems. Furthermore, but maintaining a flat hierarchy, the best ideas can flow to the top of the organization. SoHara also intends to build a strong culture of initial employees dedicated to hardwork and a team atmosphere. Although SoHara may not be able to provide the same standards in healthcare or prestige (at least initially), SoHara wants employees to feel that they can fully contribute to a unicorn startup!

Intended Employee Experience

The key goal for SoHara's employees will be Kaizen, the Japanese idea of continuous improvement. Given the small nature of the firm, employees must learn to perform many jobs at once and be cross-functional. This may mean engineering team members are participating in marketing meetings, or HR managers are speaking with engineers to get a good idea of how to choose a culture-fitting hire. In this way, employees will swiftly become highly competent contributors to SoHara.
