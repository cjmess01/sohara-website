Trends

Trend 1: Where's the Love?

As stated in the Accenture 2024 report, many consumers are feeling not cared for by firms, as these firms chase higher profits and look to cut expenses. SoHara intends to respond to this trend by building strong, ongoing relationships with downstream BESS manufacturers. In this way, SoHara can stand apart from existing firms that do not provide strong relationships and reliability.

Trend 2: Decades of Deconstruction

It used to be that advanced power utilities like BESS were limited to grids and factories. Now, more and more consumers are moving away from the traditional relationship with the energy utility, that relationship being with little power to the consumer. At present, more consumers are putting solar panels on their homes to sell energy to the grid. SoHara believes the expanding class of energy-conscious consumers may want to invest in BESS to purchase energy and store it at cheaper times, for example midday, to sell at more busy times for a profit.

Portfolio and 7Ps

SoHara intends to position the Sentinel to take advantage of two emerging trends, the growing power needs from expanding AI infrastructure and the power storage requirements of renewable energy sources.

In reflection of the premium battery safety and longevity providing ability of the Sentinel, the price will be set at a premium point of $2,000. This is a marked price increase over traditional BMS, which reflects the Sentinel's unique offerings.

The Sentinel will be sold directly to BESS manufacturers, reflecting the B2B model of the device. Targeted sales outreach to safety engineers will be pursued to generate sales.

In addition to cold calling, live demonstration promotions at battery tradeshows will be performed to gather clout and make connections into the growing BESS market.

In recognition of the complex nature of the Sentinel, SoHara will target expert engineers at established BESS manufacturers to make sales.

An important process to SoHara's success will be a lean prototype-to-production cycle. This will encompass the 3 years of R&D performed under the NSF STTR grant. In this period, a FGP will be developed in collaboration with The University of Alabama. Additionally, one or more pilot programs with a BESS manufacturer will be entered to test the integration of the device into real systems.

Physical evidence speaking to the success of the Sentinel will be gathered from the pilot programs with BESS manufacturers. Lessons learned will ease future integration projects.

Sales Channels and Social Media Strategy

The main sales channels will be direct outreach and demonstrations at sales shows to safety engineers and industry insiders.

In addition to these traditional channels, SoHara will adopt an innovative and unique social media marketing strategy. B2B companies like SoHara typically do not maintain an active social media presence; however, a strong social media footprint could be used to raise awareness for the product among end-consumers, driving the intermediate BESS manufacturers to adopt the Sentinel into their products

To fully leverage this strategy, Instagram and Linkedin accounts have been made and will begin posting trendy and flashy content to build awareness.

Intended Experience

The intended experience will be an initial purchase of a number of Sentinel units. These will be used to perform an initial integration product with a BESS line. After successful integration and validation, the remaining units will be integrated into future units of the existing line of products. From sale to final integration, SoHara intends to closly work with the customer to ensure successful use of the product.

Brands and Prices

SoHara's flagship product will be the Sentinel, a BMS with state-of-the-art SoH monitoring capabilities. In recognition of the premium nature of the product, it will be priced at $2,000.00 per unit. We intend the SoHara brand to be synonymous with safety and reliability. In the future, other permutations of the product may be offered, but there are no current plans to do so.
